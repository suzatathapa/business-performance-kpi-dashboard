# Business Requirements

## Project

Business Performance & KPI Dashboard

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to create an analytics solution that helps business stakeholders monitor sales performance, profitability, customers, products, and regional results.

The solution combines SQL, Python, and Power BI to transform transaction data into reliable reporting and actionable business insights.

## Business Questions

The analysis is designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the total revenue and profit?
2. How many orders and customers are represented in the data?
3. What is the overall profit margin?
4. What is the average order value?
5. Which products generate the most revenue?
6. Which products generate the most profit?
7. Which categories perform best?
8. Which regions generate the most revenue and profit?
9. Which customers generate the most revenue?
10. How does revenue change over time?
11. Are there significant changes or trends in monthly performance?
12. Are there data-quality issues that could affect reporting accuracy?

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard will track:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Units Sold
- Profit Margin
- Average Order Value

## Data Quality Requirements

Before analysis and reporting, the data should be checked for:

- Missing order IDs
- Missing customer IDs
- Missing order dates
- Duplicate orders
- Invalid quantities
- Invalid prices
- Invalid discounts
- Unexpected category values
- Unexpected region values

## Reporting Requirements

The Power BI dashboard should provide:

- Executive-level KPI summary
- Revenue and profit trends
- Product performance
- Category performance
- Regional performance
- Customer performance
- Interactive filters
- Clear and readable visualizations

## Expected Business Value

The project demonstrates how transaction-level data can be transformed into reliable reporting that supports performance monitoring, trend analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

It also demonstrates a repeatable workflow for data validation, transformation, analysis, visualization, and documentation.
